## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Furnished student coliving in St. Augustine — a private bedroom with an en suite bath in a real shared home at 3530 Datura, all-inclusive, built especially for flight students near KSGJ / Florida Flyers.
**What it does:** Marvento leases move-in-ready private rooms (each with its own bathroom) in a furnished shared residence. One monthly rate covers rent, electric, water, sewer, enterprise Wi-Fi, weekly housekeeping of common areas, and home maintenance. Lease terms match training or semester timelines — month-to-month through 12 months — with no SSN or domestic co-signer required, including remote holds for incoming M1 students.
**Product category:** Student housing / student apartments / student pilot housing near St. Augustine Airport (how people search). Honest product: furnished private room in a shared home (coliving), not a 300-unit apartment complex.
**Product type:** Local housing / coliving (physical inventory, finite rooms)
**Business model:** Room-month revenue at 3530. Private-room term ladder: **$1,650** month-to-month · **$1,495** (3–5 mo) · **$1,350** (6–9 mo) · **$1,195** (10–12 mo). Select shared rooms from **$850 per student**. No buy-in fees. Partner referral: **$1,000** per resident placed (paid on move-in + 30-day residency confirmation). Inventory constraint: a handful of rooms in one house — success is fill rate, term quality, and resident fit, not infinite top-of-funnel.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Training (or the semester) is blocked or degraded by housing friction — SSN/co-signer walls, unfurnished 12-month leases, unreliable Wi-Fi, long post-lesson commutes, and a listed rent that is never the real monthly cost.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Extended-stay motels near KSGJ (~$1,450/mo) — highway rooms, no kitchen, no study desk, no rest for irregular slots
- Traditional apartments (listed ~$1,450, true cost often ~$2,069) — fees, utilities, internet, furniture, SSN, co-signer, rigid 12 months
- Downtown student houses / roommate hunts — deposits, utilities split, roommate risk, shared baths
- Vacation rentals / Airbnb — tourist pricing, no training-length terms, unstable
**What it costs them:** Delayed start dates, wasted stipend, fatigue that shows up on the flight line, months of rent after the check-ride, and the humiliation of an application rejection on arrival.
**Emotional tension:** Housing panic before day one. Parents worried about motels and strangers. International students afraid the visa will clear and there will still be nowhere to sleep.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Conventional St. Augustine apartment complexes (e.g. large U.S. 1 corridor communities; student-oriented complexes such as RISE / The Landing in the local consideration set) — fall short on SSN/co-signer, furniture, hidden fees, and 12-month lock-in. Flagler-oriented downtown houses — fall short on setup friction and shared-bath roommate risk.
**Secondary:** Extended-stay motels (Travelodge / Days Inn class near KSGJ, ~$1,450) — fall short as a place to live, study, and cook. Furnished corporate housing ($1,700–$2,000+) — overpriced for a student stipend.
**Indirect:** Craigslist roommate shares, tourist Airbnbs, commuting from Jacksonville/Orlando, staying with family — fall short on certainty, commute, or rest. Do **not** treat 3540 Datura (senior home) as a student competitor or a student campaign destination.

**Pull:** Private en suite, one all-inclusive number, 8–11 min to the field, remote hold, term matched to training.

## Positioning decisions (2026-09-07)
- **Category we claim:** Training-aligned student housing (pilots first) — apartment-level privacy without apartment-level friction. Not “another complex.”
- **Search bridge:** Lead with “student apartments / student housing,” then clarify furnished private room + en suite in a shared home.
- **Beachhead ICP:** Student pilots at Florida Flyers / KSGJ (domestic + M1). Flagler and other students fill remaining rooms; they are not the paid-search headline.
- **Sibling product:** 3540 Datura is senior 55+ independent living. Separate campaigns, separate landing pages, no mixed creative.
- **Distribution theory:** Harvest intent (Search + SEO) + flight-school referrals beat lifestyle brand ads. Housing is a large, low-frequency problem per student.
