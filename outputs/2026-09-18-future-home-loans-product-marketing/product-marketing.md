## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Veteran-owned mortgage **broker** that shops 30+ lenders to get veterans and homebuyers a fair rate and a fast, no-surprises close — with the VA paperwork (including pulling your COE) handled for you.
**What it does:** Future Home Loans is a broker, not a direct lender. Instead of one bank's rate sheet, they run each borrower's file against 30+ wholesale lenders to find the best price and program, then manage the loan in-house through clear-to-close. For veterans they pull the **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** on the borrower's behalf and charge **no lender fees**. They handle purchase and refinance across FHA, VA, USDA, DSCR, HELOC, Fixed, ARM, Jumbo, and Foreign National loans.
**Product category:** Mortgage broker / VA home loans / home loans (how borrowers search: "VA loan," "mortgage broker near me," "VA loan Florida," "no down payment home loan"). Honest product: an independent broker who shops lenders — not a retail bank and not a call-center lender.
**Product type:** Local, licensed mortgage brokerage (service business; revenue per funded loan). Headquartered at 224 3rd St N, Jacksonville Beach, FL 32250 · (904) 494-6000.
**Business model:** Broker compensation per funded loan. Growth is a function of qualified applications → pre-approvals → funded loans, not raw traffic. **No lender fees for veterans** (third-party costs — appraisal, title — still apply).
**States served (13):** AL, CA, CO, FL, GA, IN, KY, NC, OH, SC, TN, TX, VA. **Florida is the current paid-marketing beachhead.**

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Getting a mortgage — especially a VA loan — through a big bank or retail lender is slow, impersonal, and priced to the lender's advantage. Borrowers feel like a number, get surprised at closing, and often aren't sure they'll even qualify.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big-name VA lenders and retail banks offer **one rate sheet** — no shopping — so the borrower rarely gets the best price
- Call-center lenders **go dark**; borrowers don't know what's happening and fear missing the closing date
- Banks bounce **non-traditional** borrowers (self-employed, investors, complex VA properties) that a broker can place
- VA borrowers get stuck on **COE / eligibility** and complex-property rules a big lender won't work through
**What it costs them:** A worse rate for 15–30 years, a blown or delayed closing, thousands in avoidable fees, and — for people who were declined — the belief that they can't own a home at all.
**Emotional tension:** "Will I even qualify?" · fear of being ghosted mid-deal · the stress of a tight closing timeline · a veteran's frustration at not being helped by the people who should understand the benefit. Recurring review line: *"Never in a million years did I think I would be able to say, 'I'm a homeowner.'"*
