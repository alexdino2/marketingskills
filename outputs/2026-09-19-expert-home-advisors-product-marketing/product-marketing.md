## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Buying or selling a home in Jacksonville is high-stakes and stressful, and most consumers' first move — a national portal — hands them off to whichever agent paid for the lead, not the best local team. Sellers fear leaving money on the table; buyers fear being ignored, outbid, or stuck with an agent who doesn't know the area; both fear a deal that falls apart at the closing table.
**Why alternatives fall short (see Competitive Landscape for mechanics):**
- **Portals (Zillow/Redfin/Homelight)** sell the consumer's attention to whichever agent bought the lead — no continuity, no accountability, no local depth
- **Solo agents** get overwhelmed at volume; calls and paperwork slip because there's no support staff behind them
- **iBuyers / instant-offer** services trade a real market price for convenience, quietly costing the seller thousands
- **Discount / flat-fee brokers** cut the service (marketing, negotiation, transaction management) that actually protects the price
**What it costs them:** Thousands left on the table (a weak sale price or an overpaid purchase), a dead or delayed deal, weeks of stress and silence, and — for relocators — buying in the wrong neighborhood.
**Emotional tension:** "Will they even call me back?" · "Am I leaving money on the table?" · "Is this deal going to fall apart?" · the relocator's anxiety of committing to a home in a city they don't yet know. Recurring review theme: relief at a process that was "smooth and stress free" and a team that "truly listens."

## Goals
**Business goal:** Sustainable, relationship-driven growth in listings and closings across NE Florida — **sellers first, then buyers** (investors in scope but not the budget focus) — without reverting to bought portal leads.
**Primary conversion action:** **Lead generation** — a form submit or tracked call from an in-footprint buyer/seller (weighted to sellers). The owned marketing engine's job is to produce leads; the **Control Tower speed-to-lead engine** then works them.
**Goal / north-star metric:** **Appointments** — booked consultations (weighted to seller/listing appointments). Optimize the funnel on lead→appointment and appointment→signed-client rate, not raw traffic. Gate the funnel to the NE-Florida footprint and screen the anti-persona.
**Funnel shape:** owned demand (SEO/Meta/Google) → **lead** (form/call) → Control Tower speed-to-lead + follow-up → **appointment** → signed client → closing → referral.

## Compliance (real estate — must-run)
- **Fair Housing:** never steer by protected class; avoid neighborhood descriptions that imply a preferred buyer profile. Meta housing ads run under the **Special Ad Category** (restricted targeting) — prefer property/location imagery.
- **Brokerage identity:** display the licensed brokerage (**JPAR City & Beach**) and required license info per Florida rules on marketing where required; EHA is a team, not an independent brokerage.
- **No guarantees:** don't guarantee a sale price, a timeline, or "over asking" as an expectation — cite past results as past results, not promises.
- **Commission:** post-NAR-settlement, present buyer-side compensation carefully; don't imply a fixed rate before a listing agreement.
- **Testimonials:** get written consent before using a named client's review in paid ads; keep claims truthful and substantiated.
- **Competitors:** competitor shortfalls in this doc are internal positioning; don't publish disparaging factual claims about named portals/brokers without verification — prefer category framing in ads.
