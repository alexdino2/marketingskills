## Company snapshot

- **One-sentence:** Marvento Homes operates furnished coliving residences in St. Augustine, FL — student housing at 3530 Datura (flight + local students) and senior independent living at 3540 Datura.
- **Primary product for this plan:** Student pilot housing — private en suite furnished rooms, all-inclusive, lease terms matched to flight training programs.
- **Other SKUs:** Shared double-occupancy rooms from $850/student; broader “student apartments” positioning; senior independent living (out of scope for this plan’s acquisition engine).
- **Status:** Live / GA — sites and inquiry forms are live. Room inventory is finite (one home, handful of rooms).
- **Domain:** marventohomes.com
- **Archetype adaptation:** Local housing / coliving (not SaaS). AARRR maps to: inquiry → lease → move-in → residency → referral → revenue per room-month.

## ICP

- **Primary ICP:** Student pilots training near KSGJ — Florida Flyers Flight Academy (4730 Casa Cola Way) and Florida Aviation Career Training; domestic and international (M1 visa) cadets; PPL through zero-to-hero career tracks.
- **Secondary:** Flagler College juniors/seniors/transfers and other St. Augustine students seeking furnished off-campus housing.
- **Buyer influences:** Parents, flight-school admissions / international student coordinators, financial-aid contacts (Sallie Mae stipend documentation).
- **Stated problem:** “I need a place near the flight school that isn’t a motel and doesn’t require an apartment gauntlet.”
- **Real problem:** Training start is blocked or degraded by housing friction — SSN/co-signer, empty unfurnished units, 12-month lock-ins, unreliable Wi-Fi, long post-lesson commute, and opaque true monthly cost.
- **What they’re buying:** Certainty — a furnished private room + private bath, one all-inclusive number, remote lease/hold (including while awaiting M1), 8–11 min to the flight line.

## Strategic posture (inferred)

- **Most important this quarter:** Fill 3530 Datura with the right student pilots (and compatible students) at healthy term lengths — not brand vanity.
- **Most important to ignore:** Senior-home acquisition campaigns mixed into student spend; broad “coliving lifestyle” brand campaigns before Search + partnerships work.
- **Constraints:** Fair Housing / Housing Special Ad Category; finite rooms (scarcity is real — do not invent waitlist numbers); not affiliated with Flagler, Florida Flyers, or Sallie Mae; honesty that this is a shared home, not a 300-unit complex.

## Market-quality gate

- **Problem size:** Large — housing determines whether training starts on time and whether rest/study supports check-rides.
- **Frequency (per student):** Low–episodic (one housing decision per training block).
- **Aggregate demand:** Recurring cohort waves from flight schools.
- **Quadrant:** Large + low personal frequency → **winnable but expensive to stay top-of-mind via brand ads alone**. Plan prioritizes **intent capture (Search/SEO)** + **partner distribution (flight schools)** + **fast activation** over awareness theater.
