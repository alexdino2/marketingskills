## 1. Executive summary

This plan optimizes for one outcome: **keep 3530 Datura full of the right student pilots (and compatible flight/local students) at healthy lease terms — without wasting spend before conversion tracking and inquiry follow-up are reliable.**

Marvento already has what most local housing operators never build: a sharp category claim (“housing built for student pilots”), a conversion page that answers real objections (no SSN/co-signer, term-matched pricing, ForeFlight Wi-Fi, 8-minute commute), a public **$1,000 partner referral fee**, and a ready-to-launch Search + Meta creative package. The gap is not messaging. The gap is **operating the funnel** — measurement, post-inquiry close rate, flight-school distribution, and shipping the content already drafted.

**Three big bets, ranked by leverage:**

1. **Harvest intent before you invent it.** Incoming pilots already search “Florida Flyers housing,” “KSGJ student housing,” and “student apartments St. Augustine.” Wire a real Google Ads conversion ID, launch the Search campaign from the existing RSA package, and publish the student-apartments SEO cluster so organic compounds under paid. Brand awareness ads are a later, smaller bet — this market is large-problem / low personal-frequency, so search + partners beat lifestyle spend.
2. **Treat flight schools as the primary distribution channel.** Admissions counselors, international student coordinators, and financial-aid contacts already send people into the motel trap. Productize the $1,000 referral offer with a one-pager, WhatsApp path, and a 24-hour SLA back to the school. One steady partner cohort fills more rooms than a month of cold Meta.
3. **Close the inquiry leak.** The product page converts curiosity into forms; forms without nurture and ops discipline convert into Travelodge. Ship the drafted 5-email / 10-day nurture, enforce human reply + WhatsApp within hours (not “eventually”), and instrument inquiry → tour/hold → lease → move-in so you know the real close rate.

**What twelve months looks like, plausibly:**

- 3530 Datura at **stable high occupancy** with a mix biased toward 6–12 month terms (better revenue predictability than pure month-to-month).
- Google Search as a **measured** channel with known cost-per-qualified-inquiry and cost-per-lease.
- At least **1–2 active flight-school referral relationships** producing predictable inbound.
- Student content hub + 4 posts live; pilot page remaining the primary conversion URL for aviation intent.
- Inquiry nurture live; reply-time and lease-close rate tracked weekly.
- Meta used sparingly for retargeting and angle tests — not as the primary acquisition engine.
- Blended CAC (ads + tools + partner fees + ops time) known well enough to decide whether to raise paid budget.

**90-day priorities:**

1. Fix Google conversion tracking (`AW-XXXXXXXXXX` → real ID); do not spend until submit events fire.
2. Launch Google Search (brand + 3 non-brand ad groups) pointed at the pilot page.
3. Publish student apartments hub + blog cluster; internally link to pilot page and `/3530-datura`.
4. Stand up inquiry nurture in an ESP; enforce human first-response SLA.
5. Ship flight-school partner kit + activate the $1,000 referral offer with 2–3 target schools.
6. Instrument a simple pipeline: Inquiry → Qualified → Hold/Lease → Move-in → Day-30 retained.

Everything else in this document sequences off those six.

## 2. Strategic frame

### What Marvento is, in one sentence

Furnished coliving homes in St. Augustine where residents rent a **private bedroom with en suite bath** in a real shared home — all-inclusive utilities, Wi-Fi, and housekeeping — with a student residence at **3530 Datura** built especially for **flight students near KSGJ / Florida Flyers**.

### The category we're claiming

Marvento is not competing as “another apartment complex.” It claims **training-aligned student pilot housing**: apartment-level privacy (private room + private bath) without apartment-level friction (furniture, deposits, guarantor, rigid 12-month lock-in, SSN).

Category frame for student pilots:

> Motels are temporary. Apartments are bureaucratic. Marvento is the furnished private room built for how pilots actually train — short commute, sleep after irregular slots, ForeFlight-grade Wi-Fi, lease length matched to the rating.

This is a **redefinition of an existing local housing category**, not a new category invention. The search language students use is still “apartments / housing / rooms.” Content and ads must **bridge**: lead with search language, clarify the product honestly (shared home, not a 300-unit leasing office).

### Who we're for (ICP, distilled)

- **Primary:** Student pilots at Florida Flyers and other KSGJ schools — PPL through commercial / zero-to-hero; domestic relocators and **M1 international** cadets.
- **Economic reality:** Often loan-financed (e.g., Sallie Mae career training loans with a living stipend); sensitive to true monthly cost and documentation.
- **Stated want:** A clean room near the airport that won’t reject their application.
- **Actual want:** Certainty before day one — remote hold, one all-inclusive number, sleep and study conditions that don’t sabotage check-rides.
- **Influencers:** Parents overseas/out-of-state; school admissions / international coordinators; financial aid.
- **Not primary (this plan):** Active adults 55+ at 3540; pure Flagler lifestyle seekers (secondary demand only when rooms would otherwise sit empty).

### The business model logic

- **Unit of revenue:** Room-month at 3530 (private tier ladder $1,195–$1,650; shared from $850/student).
- **Inventory constraint:** Finite rooms. Marketing success = **fill rate + term quality + resident fit**, not infinite top-of-funnel scale.
- **Unit economics theory:** Longer terms lower monthly rate but raise predictability and reduce vacancy churn cost. Shared rooms expand accessible price points for stipend-constrained students.
- **Acquisition theory:** High-intent Search + school referrals should dominate CAC. Content SEO compounds. Paid social auditions angles and retargets — it does not replace intent channels.
- **Compounding assets:** Pilot conversion page, cost-comparison content, partner relationships, review/testimonial library (once real residents consent), referral loop among classmates.

### Market-quality gate

| | Low frequency | High frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Large problem** | **← Marvento sits here for each student** | Best compounding quadrant |
| **Small problem** | Weakest | Habit / price churn |

Housing for flight training is a **large, episodic** problem. Implication: do not build the plan around constant brand presence. Build it around **being present at decision time** (search, school desk, parent WhatsApp) and **closing fast**.

### Brand voice (non-negotiables)

Derived from live site + prior packages (no formal brand doc — flag in §13):

**YES**
- Precise distances and times (3.9 miles, 8–11 minutes)
- All-inclusive pricing with the term ladder visible
- Name the frictions: SSN, co-signer, furniture, hidden apartment fees
- Honest product: private room in a shared home; rooms are limited
- Equal Housing Opportunity on ads and pages

**NO**
- Pretending to be a large apartment complex
- Invented occupancy, waitlists, or testimonials
- Promising visa approval, check-ride outcomes, or guaranteed availability
- Affiliation claims with Florida Flyers, Flagler, Sallie Mae, or USAHS
- Mixing senior-home creative into student campaigns
- Fair Housing violations (targeting/excluding protected classes; people-as-demographic targeting in Housing ads)

**CTA pattern:** Check Availability / Reserve a Room / We reply within 24 hours — helpful, not high-pressure countdown gimmicks.

## 5. Activation

*How an aware prospect has a first valued experience — here: **qualified inquiry → hold/lease certainty**, not a SaaS “aha.”*

### Current state

- Strong page → form path on pilot URL (training program + timing fields already qualify leads).
- Claimed 24-hour availability confirmation.
- Nurture sequence drafted (5 emails / 10 days) covering cost, paperwork, proximity, shared rooms — not confirmed live in an ESP.
- Move-in product experience (furnished, Wi-Fi, desk, quiet hours) is strong once leased; the digital gap is everything before keys.
- Homepage generic form and pilot form may create two inboxes — consolidate routing.

### Activation definition (lock this)

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| Inquiry | Form submit or qualified inbound call/WhatsApp |
| Qualified | Training near KSGJ / student fit + timing within ~6 months (or M1 wait) |
| Activated | **Remote room hold signed OR lease signed** (choose one legal instrument and use it everywhere) |
| Moved in | Keys + Day-1 checklist complete |
| Retained-30 | Still in residence at day 30 (also unlocks partner referral payout) |

Leading indicator that predicts Activated: **median hours to first human reply**.

### The plan

1. **Human SLA:** First reply in **<4 business hours** during intake season (same-day goal for “Immediately / Within 1–2 months” form answers). Use WhatsApp/SMS for international students — the form already collects Phone/WhatsApp.
2. **Reply script (first touch):** Confirm receipt → restate term interest → ask move-in week + private vs shared → offer hold path → link to 3530 photos. Do not send a brochure dump.
3. **Ship nurture** from `emails/inquire-nurture.md` — trigger when no tour/hold within 24h of submit:
   - Email 1 immediate confirmation  
   - Day 2 true-cost table  
   - Day 4 no-SSN / remote lease  
   - Day 7 proximity + study/sleep  
   - Day 10 shared-room / last nudge  
   *(Skill: `emails`)*
4. **Concierge activation for M1 / overseas** — single checklist: documents, stipend tip (point to 3530 Sallie Mae section), arrival day, Wi-Fi/ForeFlight ready, who meets them. *(Idea #95 adapted)*
5. **Tour path** — Domestic/local: fixed tour windows or calendar link. Remote: Loom/video walkthrough of 3530 (produce in Q2 if capacity).
6. **Parent/advisor loop** — When inquiry mentions parent or school, CC/forward a short “what’s included + all-inclusive number” note; parents often hold the veto.
7. **Form CRO** — Keep training program + timing fields (they qualify). Test shorter forms only after conversion tracking works. *(Skill: `cro`)*
8. **Fail states to log weekly:** no reply, price objection, wanted full apartment, chose motel, timing slipped, school housing elsewhere.

### 90-day activation outcomes

- Median first-reply time tracked and under SLA.
- Nurture live; at least some stalled leads reactivating via reply.
- Baseline inquiry → Activated rate known (even if ugly).

### 12-month activation outlook

- Playbooks by segment: M1 remote, domestic loan-stipend, short add-on rating, shared-room price-sensitive.
- Optional booking calendar + e-sign hold to reduce WhatsApp back-and-forth.

### Skills + tools

`emails`, `copywriting`, `onboarding` (adapted to move-in), `cro`, `signup` patterns adapted · ESP, SMS/WhatsApp, simple CRM or Sheet pipeline, e-sign (optional).

## 6. Retention

*How a resident stays through their training term and becomes a renewer / referrer.*

### Current state

- Product retention levers already designed in: quiet hours, blackout curtains, housekeeping, study desk, kitchen, yard, short commute.
- No evidence of lifecycle messaging during residency or at term-end.
- House culture will make or break word-of-mouth inside flight-school cohorts (one bad roommate conflict story travels fast).

### The plan

1. **Day-3 and Day-30 check-ins** — Ops WhatsApp/SMS: Wi-Fi/ForeFlight OK? Sleep OK? Any house issue? Fix fast. Support-as-marketing. *(Idea #135)*
2. **Issue log** — Date, room, issue, time-to-fix. Patterns (Wi-Fi, noise, AC) become product and ad honesty fuel.
3. **Term-end renewal / extension flow** — At **45 and 21 days** before term end: extend at current ladder, switch private↔shared, or nominate a classmate for the room (feeds §7).
4. **House norms as brand** — One-pager on quiet hours, guests, kitchen, parking. Reduces churn-by-conflict.
5. **Win-back for alumni** — Add-on ratings (multi, instrument) often bring pilots back; keep a light alumni list for month-to-month inventory. *(Idea #52)*
6. **Do not over-email residents** — They are studying to fly. Retention is mostly ops excellence + timely renewal asks.
7. **Permissioned testimonials** — After Day-30, ask for a Google review + optional quote. Never invent. *(Idea #129)*

### 90-day retention outcomes

- Check-in cadence running; issue log exists.
- Renewal outreach template live for the earliest expiring leases.
- At least one permissioned review request process documented.

### 12-month retention outlook

- Extension rate by original term band known.
- Alumni reactivation contributing measurable month-to-month fills.
- House norms refined from real conflict patterns (not theory).

### Skills + tools

`emails`, `customer-research` (VoC from check-ins) · ESP or WhatsApp templates · ops Sheet · Google Business Profile.

## 7. Referral

*How retained residents and schools bring the next cohort.*

### Current state

- Homepage already offers **$1,000 referral fee** to discharge planners, senior placement advisors, **flight school advisors**, and housing partners — paid on move-in + 30-day residency confirmation.
- Pilot page invites admissions counselors / international coordinators to call about cohort housing.
- No public playbook, tracking sheet, or leave-behind seen in materials — the offer is ahead of the operating system.

### Why this is Bet #2

Flight-school staff see every incoming cadet’s housing panic before Google Ads does. A working referral desk turns Marvento into default advice (“don’t do the motel — call these people”) which is the highest-trust acquisition path in a low-frequency category.

### The plan

1. **Partner kit (Now):** 1-page PDF — audience, inclusions, pricing ladder, no-SSN/remote hold, fee terms ($1,000 on move-in + 30 days), contact, Equal Housing line, “not affiliated with [school].” *(Skills: `copywriting`, `referrals`)*
2. **Priority accounts (in order):**
   1. Florida Flyers — admissions + international student coordinator  
   2. Florida Aviation Career Training — housing/admissions contact  
   3. Any other Part 141 operators on KSGJ field  
3. **Outreach sequence:** Intro email → kit PDF → offer 20-min call → site tour invite for staff → agree referral attribution method.
4. **Resident referral:** Classmate bonus (e.g., $200–$400 rent credit) — smaller than partner fee; track separately. Layer two-sided referrals (#137) in Q2 once partners work.
5. **Attribution:** Add “Who referred you?” to forms; UTM `utm_source=partner&utm_campaign={school}`; Sheet columns: Referred → Moved in → Day-30 → Fee paid / date.
6. **Compliance:** Confirm each school’s gift/referral policy before promising fees. Keep Fair Housing: sell the housing product, not a preferred nationality.
7. **Soft launch moment:** When first partner is live, treat it as a mini-launch — update FAQ, train leasing on “partner lead” priority SLA.

### 90-day referral outcomes

- Kit in hand; ≥5 outreach touches; ≥1 active referrer producing leads.
- Form captures referral source on 100% of new inquiries (field required or strongly prompted).

### 12-month referral outlook

- 1–2 schools with repeat cohort flow.
- Resident classmate loop contributing fill between school cohorts.
- Referral CAC (fee + ops time) compared honestly to Search CPA.

### Skills + tools

`referrals`, `copywriting`, `launch` (soft partner launch) · Sheets, email/WhatsApp, Doc/PDF.

## 13. Measurement, RACI, open decisions, appendix

### North-star metric

**Occupied room-nights (or occupancy %) at 3530 Datura among student-fit residents.**

### Leading indicators

| Stage | Leading indicator |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | Qualified inquiries / week; Search cost-per-inquiry; organic form submits |
| Activation | Median first-reply time; inquiry → hold/lease rate |
| Retention | Day-30 residency confirmation; extension rate |
| Referral | Partner-attributed leases; fee payables |
| Revenue | ADR / term mix; vacancy weeks; blended CAC |

### Weekly cadence

30-minute weekly review: pipeline counts, reply-time, ad CPA, vacancies in next 30/60 days. Flattening occupancy or rising reply-time = trigger to shift effort (not to “post more and hope”).

### RACI (roles)

| Workstream | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paid Search / Meta | Ads owner / contractor | Founder | Leasing | — |
| Site publish / SEO | Web | Founder | Content | Leasing |
| Inquiry reply / nurture | Leasing | Founder | — | Partners |
| School partnerships | Founder | Founder | Leasing | Finance |
| Pricing / discounts | Founder | Founder | Leasing | — |
| Fair Housing compliance | Founder | Founder | Ads | All |

### Open decisions (ranked by impact)

1. **Blended CAC and current occupancy / term mix** — blocks budget science.
2. **Who owns inbox/WhatsApp and what is the true reply-time today?**
3. **ESP choice** for nurture (Resend vs Customer.io vs Mailchimp vs other).
4. **Monthly paid budget ceiling** for Q1 after tracking works.
5. **Which flight schools are legally/operationally open to $1,000 referral fees?**
6. **Canonical public phone number** (surfaces currently disagree).
7. **Is `/student-apartments-st-augustine` approved to publish as drafted?**
8. **Formal brand voice doc** — create via `product-marketing` skill?
9. **Funding / growth intent** — stay one home vs expand inventory in 12 months?
10. **Analytics access** — GA4 / Ads accounts available to whoever executes?
