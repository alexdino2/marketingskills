## 1. Executive summary

This plan optimizes for one outcome: **keep 3530 Datura full of the right student pilots (and compatible flight/local students) at healthy lease terms — without wasting spend before conversion tracking and inquiry follow-up are reliable.**

Marvento already has what most local housing operators never build: a sharp category claim (“housing built for student pilots”), a conversion page that answers real objections (no SSN/co-signer, term-matched pricing, ForeFlight Wi-Fi, 8-minute commute), a public **$1,000 partner referral fee**, and a ready-to-launch Search + Meta creative package. The gap is not messaging. The gap is **operating the funnel** — measurement, post-inquiry close rate, flight-school distribution, and shipping the content already drafted.

**Three big bets, ranked by leverage:**

1. **Harvest intent before you invent it.** Incoming pilots already search “Florida Flyers housing,” “KSGJ student housing,” and “student apartments St. Augustine.” Wire a real Google Ads conversion ID, launch the Search campaign from the existing RSA package, and publish the student-apartments SEO cluster so organic compounds under paid. Brand awareness ads are a later, smaller bet — this market is large-problem / low personal-frequency, so search + partners beat lifestyle spend.
2. **Treat flight schools as the primary distribution channel.** Admissions counselors, international student coordinators, and financial-aid contacts already send people into the motel trap. Productize the $1,000 referral offer with a one-pager, WhatsApp path, and a 24-hour SLA back to the school. One steady partner cohort fills more rooms than a month of cold Meta.
3. **Close the inquiry leak.** The product page converts curiosity into forms; forms without nurture and ops discipline convert into Travelodge. Ship the drafted 5-email / 10-day nurture, enforce human reply + WhatsApp within hours (not “eventually”), and instrument inquiry → tour/hold → lease → move-in so you know the real close rate.

**What twelve months looks like, plausibly:**

- 3530 Datura at **stable high occupancy** with a mix biased toward 6–12 month terms (better revenue predictability than pure month-to-month).
- Google Search as a **measured** channel with known cost-per-qualified-inquiry and cost-per-lease.
- At least **1–2 active flight-school referral relationships** producing predictable inbound.
- Student content hub + 4 posts live; pilot page remaining the primary conversion URL for aviation intent.
- Inquiry nurture live; reply-time and lease-close rate tracked weekly.
- Meta used sparingly for retargeting and angle tests — not as the primary acquisition engine.
- Blended CAC (ads + tools + partner fees + ops time) known well enough to decide whether to raise paid budget.

**90-day priorities:**

1. Fix Google conversion tracking (`AW-XXXXXXXXXX` → real ID); do not spend until submit events fire.
2. Launch Google Search (brand + 3 non-brand ad groups) pointed at the pilot page.
3. Publish student apartments hub + blog cluster; internally link to pilot page and `/3530-datura`.
4. Stand up inquiry nurture in an ESP; enforce human first-response SLA.
5. Ship flight-school partner kit + activate the $1,000 referral offer with 2–3 target schools.
6. Instrument a simple pipeline: Inquiry → Qualified → Hold/Lease → Move-in → Day-30 retained.

Everything else in this document sequences off those six.
