## 4. Acquisition

*How strangers become aware of Marvento student pilot housing.*

### Current state

- Live organic pages carry most awareness today.
- Paid Search/Meta packaged but launch-gated.
- Partner channel stated but not productized.
- Social handles `@marventohomes` exist; calendar drafted.

### The plan (numbered moves)

1. **Instrumentation first** — Real Google Ads conversion on “Check Availability”; GA4/GTM events for form submit, click-to-call, WhatsApp if used. *(Skill: `analytics-tracking`; Tools: GTM, Google Ads, GA4)*
2. **Launch Search** — Brand campaign + AG1 Florida Flyers/KSGJ + AG2 student apartments + AG3 no-SSN/furnished/remote lease, per existing UPLOAD.md. Location: US (+ interest in St. Johns) — **do not geo-lock only to people already in St. Augustine** (M1/out-of-state converters). *(Skills: `ads`, `ad-creative`)*
3. **Publish SEO cluster** — `/student-apartments-st-augustine` + 4 posts; link aggressively to pilot page for aviation queries and 3530 for property proof. *(Skills: `content-strategy`, `copywriting`, `ai-seo`, `seo-audit`)*
4. **Partner pipeline** — Target Florida Flyers, Florida Aviation Career Training, and any other KSGJ schools; leave-behind PDF; $1,000 fee process. *(Skill: `referral-program` / partnerships motion in `referrals`)*
5. **Meta Housing test** — Concept 01 always-on; 02–04 as angle test; property-only imagery; Special Ad Category. Kill on submit rate, not thumbstop. *(Skills: `ads`, `ad-creative`)*
6. **Retarget non-submitters** with **shared-room from $850** offer (different offer, same audience) — do not hammer the $1,195 private creative harder.

### Skipped (with reason)

- TikTok-as-primary, podcast ads, LinkedIn ads, conference circuits — wrong ROI shape for finite local inventory.
- Aggressive “coliving lifestyle” brand campaigns before Search/partners prove fill.
- Any creative that implies affiliation with named schools.

### 90-day acquisition outcomes

- Search live with ≥30 conversion signals toward learning (or clear kill if zero qualified inquiries).
- Content hub indexed; early impressions on apartment-intent queries.
- ≥5 partner conversations; ≥1 signed referral understanding.

`ads`, `ad-creative`, `seo-audit`, `ai-seo`, `content-strategy`, `copywriting`, `social-content`, `analytics-tracking` · GTM, Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Search Console, GA4 · Composio only if Sheets/Slack ops need it later.
