## 7. Referral

*How retained residents and schools bring the next cohort.*

### Current state

- Homepage already offers **$1,000 referral fee** to discharge planners, senior placement advisors, **flight school advisors**, and housing partners — paid on move-in + 30-day residency confirmation.
- Pilot page invites admissions counselors / international coordinators to call about cohort housing.
- No public playbook, tracking sheet, or leave-behind seen in materials — the offer is ahead of the operating system.

### Why this is Bet #2

Flight-school staff see every incoming cadet’s housing panic before Google Ads does. A working referral desk turns Marvento into default advice (“don’t do the motel — call these people”) which is the highest-trust acquisition path in a low-frequency category.

### The plan

1. **Partner kit (Now):** 1-page PDF — audience, inclusions, pricing ladder, no-SSN/remote hold, fee terms ($1,000 on move-in + 30 days), contact, Equal Housing line, “not affiliated with [school].” *(Skills: `copywriting`, `referrals`)*
2. **Priority accounts (in order):**
   1. Florida Flyers — admissions + international student coordinator  
   2. Florida Aviation Career Training — housing/admissions contact  
   3. Any other Part 141 operators on KSGJ field  
3. **Outreach sequence:** Intro email → kit PDF → offer 20-min call → site tour invite for staff → agree referral attribution method.
4. **Resident referral:** Classmate bonus (e.g., $200–$400 rent credit) — smaller than partner fee; track separately. Layer two-sided referrals (#137) in Q2 once partners work.
5. **Attribution:** Add “Who referred you?” to forms; UTM `utm_source=partner&utm_campaign={school}`; Sheet columns: Referred → Moved in → Day-30 → Fee paid / date.
6. **Compliance:** Confirm each school’s gift/referral policy before promising fees. Keep Fair Housing: sell the housing product, not a preferred nationality.
7. **Soft launch moment:** When first partner is live, treat it as a mini-launch — update FAQ, train leasing on “partner lead” priority SLA.

### 90-day referral outcomes

- Kit in hand; ≥5 outreach touches; ≥1 active referrer producing leads.
- Form captures referral source on 100% of new inquiries (field required or strongly prompted).

### 12-month referral outlook

- 1–2 schools with repeat cohort flow.
- Resident classmate loop contributing fill between school cohorts.
- Referral CAC (fee + ops time) compared honestly to Search CPA.

### Skills + tools

`referrals`, `copywriting`, `launch` (soft partner launch) · Sheets, email/WhatsApp, Doc/PDF.
