## 8. Revenue

*Monetization, packaging, and inventory yield — not SaaS expansion ARR.*

### Current pricing (live)

| Term | Private room |
|---|---|
| Month-to-month | $1,650/mo |
| 3–5 months | $1,495/mo |
| 6–9 months | $1,350/mo |
| 10–12 months | $1,195/mo |
| Shared (select rooms) | from $850/student |

All-inclusive: furnished en suite, utilities, Wi-Fi, weekly housekeeping. No buy-in fees.

### The plan

1. **Yield goal:** Prefer filling with **6–12 month** career-track and CPL students when demand allows; use month-to-month to plug gaps, not as the default pitch.
2. **Shared-room as stipend accessibility wedge** — Lead with private; offer shared in nurture/retarget when price is the blocker (already in ad plan).
3. **Vacancy cost > discount panic** — Model the cost of an empty room-week vs a short-term rate cut; decide a written discount policy (who can approve).
4. **Partner fee as COGS** — $1,000/placement must sit inside CAC math; do not treat it as “free growth.”
5. **Senior home separation** — Do not dilute student messaging to chase senior ADR in the same campaigns.
6. **Future (Q3+):** Second student home or more rooms is a step-function; marketing plan assumes **one-home inventory** until ops says otherwise.
