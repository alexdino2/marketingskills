## 10. 12-month outlook

Quarterly milestones tied to **capacity unlocks** (cash from occupancy / decision to fund paid), not a VC round narrative — adjust if Marvento is fundraising.

### Q1 (Days 1–90) — Bedrock

- Tracking live; Search live; content published; nurture live; partner kit live.
- Baseline: inquiries/week, reply time, lease close rate, occupancy %.

### Q2 — Prove unit economics

- Know blended CAC and cost-per-lease.
- 1–2 referral partners producing repeat inbound.
- Light Meta only if Search+partners don’t fully cover vacancy risk.
- Optional: room-tour video; competitor comparison pages for RISE / motel patterns (honest, non-defamatory).

### Q3 — Compounding

- SEO refreshing top posts; programmatic only if multi-property or city expansion justifies it (likely **skip** on one home).
- Alumni / returning add-on-rating playbook.
- If expanding inventory: begin second-home GTM using this plan’s playbook.

### Q4 — Yield & resilience

- Pricing ladder review from term-mix data.
- Enrollment-season calendar (flight school start dates) mapped to ad budgets.
- Annual “true cost of student housing” refresh as linkable asset.

### Capability unlocks

| If you unlock… | You can add… |
|---|---|
| Reliable conversion tracking + ESP | Paid Search confidently |
| ~$2–5K/mo paid budget | Meta tests + Search scale |
| Ops capacity for partners | Multi-school referral program |
| Second property | City-level SEO + larger paid |
