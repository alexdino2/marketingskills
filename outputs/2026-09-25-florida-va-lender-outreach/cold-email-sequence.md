## Market context (as of Sept 26, 2026)

What your prospects are living through right now, and how the messaging responds to it.

| Condition | What it means for a Florida VA lender | Messaging response |
|---|---|---|
| Fed hiked 25 bps on Sept 16 (to 3.75–4.00%) and signaled more | Leadership is in cost-control mode, and marketing budgets are under review | Sell **efficiency**: cost per funded loan, fixing leaks before buying traffic. Don't pitch "spend more." |
| 30-year fixed back above 7% (Freddie Mac 7.03%; daily index 7.24%, a 52-week high) | The refinance pipeline is gone and purchase is the only volume left | Frame VA **purchase** as the segment to own. Never promise or predict lower rates. |
| Purchase applications ~19% below a year ago; MBA cut its 2026 refi forecast to ~$700B | Fewer borrowers, and every lender is chasing them | Exclusive, owned demand beats shared marketplace leads when volume is scarce. |
| Florida single-family inventory tight (~4.9 months), median $415K and up ~1% YoY | Purchase deals still close, and speed and certainty win offers | Speed-to-lead and a fast site matter more, not less. |
| Florida condos distressed (12+ months supply, prices down, insurance ~181% above the national average) | VA condo deals are harder (VA project approval, special assessments) | Keep the proof and examples on **single-family** VA purchase. |

**Why VA holds up in this market:** $0 down and no monthly mortgage insurance make VA the purchase option that best absorbs higher rates for eligible buyers. That's the case for a lender to put its marketing into VA now. Keep this in B2B framing (lender to lender). It's not consumer rate advertising.

**Do not say:** "rates are coming down," "lock in before rates rise," "refinance later when rates drop," or any specific rate/APR. They're predictions you can't back, and in a consumer ad they're trigger terms.

## Using the success graphic

The graphic is your strongest proof. Put it where it's seen after someone engages, not in the first cold touch.

| Where | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Email 2 link** | Host it on a simple Digital Optimus page (graphic + three lines of context + "Get your Market Blueprint" button). Link to it as `{{results_link}}`. | Images and attachments in a first cold email hurt deliverability. A link keeps email 1 plain and lets you see who clicks, which is a buying signal. |
| **Reply follow-up** | Anyone who replies to any email gets the graphic inline with their Market Blueprint. | By then they've opted in, so an image helps instead of hurting. |
| **Market Blueprint** | Page 1 of the blueprint: "What this looked like for a Jacksonville VA lender." | Anchors the free deliverable in real results. |
| **LinkedIn** | 1) Post it on your profile the week the sequence starts. 2) Send a connection request to each prospect with no pitch. 3) After they accept, DM a link to the post. | A second channel. Prospects who see the post before email 2 recognize your name. |
| **Sales call / proposal** | Opening slide. | Proof first, then the offer. |

**Before using it, check the graphic for:**
- **The client's name or logo.** You chose "a Jacksonville VA lender," so the graphic has to match. Remove Future Home Loans' name, logo, brand colors and any recognizable screenshots.
- **Date ranges on every stat.** "2× lead rate in week one (Sept 2026)" is credible. A number with no date looks like a claim.
- **Rate or APR figures.** Remove them. They date fast and would clash with the market framing.
- **The under-$50-per-lead number.** Only show it if it's documented. Otherwise use "+180% leads at flat cost per lead."
