# Florida VA Lender Outreach — Cold Email Sequence

**Sender:** Alex Destino, Digital Optimus
**Audience:** Owners, CEOs, and marketing/lending leaders at Florida-licensed VA lenders and credit unions
**Ask:** Reply to get a free Market Blueprint (their Florida VA market, one business day)
**Proof (anonymized):** "a Jacksonville VA lender"

> **Before sending**
> - Replace `[$XX]` with the documented cost per lead and the date range it covers. Don't send the "<$50/lead" claim until you have it in writing.
> - Include a physical mailing address and a one-line opt-out in every email (CAN-SPAM).
> - Send from a secondary domain that's already warmed up, not digitaloptimus.com itself.

## Variants by segment

| Segment | Change |
|---|---|
| **Credit unions** (PenAir, CCU of Florida) | Say "member" instead of "borrower". In email 1, name the local base: "veterans and service members around NAS Pensacola / Patrick SFB". Drop the marketplace angle in email 3 and replace it with "members financing elsewhere because a national lender answered first". |
| **Marketing leaders** (Director of Marketing, Field Marketing Mgr) | In email 2, lead with the attribution join: "cost per funded VA loan, not just cost per lead". |
| **CEO/President** | Keep every email under 90 words. Cut the second paragraph of email 1 if needed. |
