## LinkedIn lead-gen form (if not sending to the page)

- **Headline:** Book a free strategy call
- **Details:** 20 minutes. Live look at your rankings, competitors, and what ChatGPT says about your market. Action plan and pricing if we are a fit. Half of these calls end with a fix you can make yourself.
- **Fields:** First name, last name, work email, company, job title, phone (optional)
- **Confirmation:** Alex will meet you on the calendar slot. Bring nothing. If you have monthly lead count, close rate, and marketing spend, the call gets sharper.
- **Thank-you URL:** https://digitaloptimus.com/schedule-discovery-call (in case the form does not embed the calendar)
- **Hidden / hidden custom:** utm_campaign, utm_content

Audience notes (playbook): cold 50k–300k members. Job function + seniority (Manager+) rather than a long title list. Exclude Entry and Unpaid. Audience Expansion OFF. US only for the first campaign (do not mix cheaper geos).

- [ ] LinkedIn Audience Expansion OFF
