## Headline findings (SE Ranking US, Sep 2026 — estimates)

- **Paid footprint not observable.** SE Ranking captured **zero** paid records for future.loans — no third-party tool can see into either Google Ads account. The brief prescribes Google's own surfaces (Transparency Center, Auction Insights, MCC/read access, GAQL) to get real data.
- **Brand + Jacksonville geo = organic already #1.** Brand term "future home loans" alone ≈ **12% of all organic traffic**; core Jax mortgage-broker terms sit #1 in the local pack. Paid here largely **re-buys free clicks** → brand-exclude.
- **VA-intent = organic is weak (page 3–8).** Future ranks for huge-volume VA queries but captures almost no organic traffic. VA-Florida is the growth goal and the **clean lane for coordinated paid to dominate** — and the most likely place two uncoordinated accounts collide.

## The recommendation

Reframe from "who controls Google Ads (and the 20%)" to **"who owns acquisition orchestration."** Get both accounts analyzed together; establish one acquisition plan, one measurement framework, and clear ownership of audiences / keywords / products / geographies; prove **incrementality** (incremental funded-loan value) before scaling either account.

## What's evidence vs. what still needs account access

**Evidence (this brief):** organic footprint and the brand/geo/VA lane map from SE Ranking; the mechanics of overlap and orchestration; the visibility playbook.
**Needs account access (not analyzed here):** the second account's actual keywords, geos, budgets, bidding, conversion actions, search terms, creatives, and live CPLs; de-duplicated CRM lead flow.
