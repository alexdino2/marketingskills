## The category claim

**Digital Optimus helps local operators own large, long-tail purchase cycles** — the months-long journeys where people buy homes and cars — so national portals stop sitting between the buyer and the local expert.

Home buying and car buying are not campaigns. They are cycles: research, neighborhoods or models, financing, comparison, local trust, then a booked appointment. Demand is long-tail. A few head terms get the traffic; hundreds of scenario and place intents get the closings.

We instrument the cycle, fix the constraint holding booked business back, and make you the name that comes back — in Google, in ChatGPT, and in AI Overviews — when someone in your market is ready to talk to a human.

## One-pager narrative

### The problem

A home purchase or a car purchase unfolds over months. Buyers ask dozens of questions before they ask for your name. National sites answer the generic ones and capture the relationship. Local operators show up late — or buy the same lead back from the portal that took it.

Agencies usually respond with a retainer: content calendar, monthly report, more ads into the same leaky site. Nobody owns the cycle. Nobody can say which part of the spend produced a closing.

### What we do differently

We treat the purchase as a **cycle**, not a campaign.

1. **Map the cycle** — the intents, places, products, and AI answers between first research and booked appointment in your market.
2. **Find the constraint** — speed, rankings, forms, follow-up, or paid waste. Fix the cheapest one first.
3. **Own the long tail** — neighborhood, scenario, and product searches where a local answer beats a national portal.
4. **Get booked** — visibility only counts when it becomes a calendar slot from someone in your service area.

### Proof (named, windowed — do not detach)

- **Future Home Loans** — 2× lead rate in one week after load time 5.2s → 2.9s; +180% lead volume at flat CPL over the full program.
- **Expert Home Advisors** — +300% organic leads in six months; top three for 27 local keywords above Zillow and Realtor.com.
- **North Avenue Capital** — $750M prospect demand identified; +85% site conversion in nine months.

### Offer

Free Custom Growth Strategy call (~20 minutes, founder-led) or the written Market Blueprint (15 pages, one business day). Most engagements $4,500–$13,000/month. Ninety days, then month to month.

## Words to use / avoid

**Use:** cycle, long tail, months not days, own the market, booked appointment, constraint, instrument first, local operator, portals, get found / chosen / booked.

**Avoid:** full-funnel (vague), always-on brand (agency-speak), innovate / disrupt, "leads" without defining form or call, promising to beat Zillow nationally, any rate/APR/payment language in Digital Optimus ads (you sell marketing, not loans).
