### Problem strip (one idea)

**Headline:** Portals own the head of the cycle. You buy the scraps.

**Body:** Buyers research for months before they ask for a name. National sites answer the generic questions and capture the relationship. Agencies respond with retainers and reports. Nobody owns the long tail that actually converts — until you pay the portal for the lead they took.

### Method (keep three steps, cycle language)

**Headline:** Three things we do, in cycle order

Get found across the cycle. Get chosen when they're comparing. Get booked when they're ready for a human. We start at the biggest leak, because more traffic into a site that does not convert only costs you more.

1. **Get found — local and AI search**  
   The long-tail keywords and AI answers your buyers actually use while they're still deciding.
2. **Get chosen — paid search and social**  
   Campaigns scoped to the ZIPs and intents you can serve, written for people ready to talk.
3. **Get booked — conversion and follow-up**  
   Page speed, form friction, speed-to-lead. Future Home Loans: load time 5.2s → 2.9s, lead rate 2× in one week.

### Who we work with

**Headline:** We specialize in cycles where the purchase takes months

**Home buying (primary)**

**Wealth decisions around the home (adjacent)**

**Car buying (next)**
