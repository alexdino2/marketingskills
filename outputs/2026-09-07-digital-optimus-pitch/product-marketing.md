# Product Marketing Context

**Company:** Digital Optimus (digitaloptimus.com)

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Performance marketing for large, long-tail purchase cycles — home buying first, car buying next — so local operators own demand portals usually capture.
**What it does:** Digital Optimus instruments a local market and funnel, finds the constraint holding booked business back, then runs get-found / get-chosen / get-booked work (local + AI search, paid, conversion, follow-up) until visibility becomes appointments and closings. Engagements are founder-led; reporting joins spend to CRM outcomes.
**Product category:** Performance marketing / local demand generation for high-consideration purchases (shelf buyers search: mortgage marketing agency, real estate marketing agency, local SEO for lenders).
**Product type:** Agency / professional services
**Business model:** Monthly retainer typically $4,500–$13,000; no setup fee; 90 days then month-to-month. Entry offers: free Custom Growth Strategy call (~20 min) or Market Blueprint (15-page written market breakdown, one business day).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Independent mortgage lenders and producing branches; real estate teams and brokerages; (adjacent) RIAs / advisory firms; (next cycle) auto dealers and auto finance. Geo beachhead: Jacksonville / Northeast Florida, then Orlando and Tampa, then other metros after a scan.
**Decision-makers:** Lender/branch owners and marketing directors; team leads and managing brokers; advisory principals; later dealer GMs / marketing managers.
**Primary use case:** Own local demand across a months-long purchase cycle instead of renting end-of-funnel leads from national aggregators.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Stop paying portals to buy back your own market
- Get found for the long-tail intents that convert (neighborhood, scenario, product)
- Trace marketing spend to booked appointments and closings
**Use cases:**
- Lender losing borrowers to rate-table sites and slow site UX
- Real estate team whose listings rank on Zillow before the team's site
- Firm with agency reporting that stops at clicks and rankings
- (Future) Dealer/auto finance wanting the same cycle ownership playbook

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Large purchases (home, car) unfold over months. Demand is long-tail. National portals own the head of the cycle and the relationship; local operators show up late or buy the lead back.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Portal lead marketplaces — you rent the end of a cycle someone else owns
- Retainer agencies — content calendars and reports without a join to closings
- "More ads" first — traffic into an unfixed constraint wastes budget
**What it costs them:** Inflated CPL, lost local pack/AI visibility, months of spend with no honest answer on what produced a closing
**Emotional tension:** Fatigue with retainers that never produced a closing; distrust of agency decks; fear of compliance mistakes in regulated categories

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local SEO / PPC agencies selling to lenders and agents — fall short when they report traffic/rankings without cycle ownership or CRM join
**Secondary:** In-house marketing hires — fall short on specialized local+AI search systems and multi-market instrumentation
**Indirect:** Zillow, Realtor.com, LendingTree / rate-table portals — fall short for the operator (by design); they monetize the gap between buyer and local expert

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Cycle framing: own the long tail of months-long purchases, not "run some SEO"
- Instrument-first: find the constraint before selling a bigger scope
- Named, windowed proof in home-adjacent verticals
- AI / Answer Engine visibility as part of get-found (not a buzzword bolt-on)
- Explicit lead and appointment definitions on day one
**How we do it differently:** Measure market + funnel → fix cheapest constraint → get found / chosen / booked → scale only what booked business in 90 days
**Why that's better:** Spend follows return; operators build owned demand instead of renting portal leftovers
**Why customers choose us:** Founder-led honesty, Jacksonville market fluency, proof that local can beat portals on converting intents

| Can you beat Zillow nationally? | No on head terms. Yes on local converting intents — EHA top three for 27 keywords above Zillow. |

**Anti-persona:** National brands chasing head terms only; anyone wanting guaranteed first-page rankings in two weeks; businesses outside long-cycle local purchases who want generic "social media management"; buyers who will not share CRM/closing data.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Retainers without closings; portal dependency; unattributable spend
**Pull:** Constraint-first plan; owned long-tail visibility; booked-appointment accountability
**Habit:** Monthly portal invoices and agency PDFs feel like "doing marketing"
**Anxiety:** Switching costs, ranking dips, compliance risk, another agency that overpromises
