**Used on-page claims only:** $0 down on most VA purchases; no PMI; we pull your COE; no lender fees for veterans (third-party costs still apply); 14-day average close; 24–48 hour approval; veteran-owned; NMLS #1621953; Equal Housing Lender; 3,000+ five-star reviews; $3.2B funded. Eligibility: veterans, eligible service members, surviving spouses.

**Not used in ads (stale or needs heavier disclosure):** today’s 6.0% rate / 6.446% APR (scenario-dated Sep 1, 2026 — would require full APR copy in every ad); 98% approval rate (method not documented on-page); individual borrower reviews (none of the published quotes are explicitly VA-loan stories).

## Compliance (must-run)

- Meta: **Housing** Special Ad Category. Do not use detailed targeting that excludes protected classes.
- Keep NMLS #1621953 and Equal Housing Lender on the creative or in primary text.
- “No lender fees” always paired with “third-party costs (appraisal, title) still apply.”
- “$0 down” always qualified as “most VA purchase loans” in primary text (on-image shorthand is OK if body copy qualifies it).
- Do not guarantee approval. Do not imply VA loans are exclusive to one lender — they are a federal benefit Future Home Loans originates.
- Imagery is property-only (no people) to reduce Fair Housing “ad targeting by demographic portrayal” risk.
