## Section: FAQ

### Is this an apartment complex?
No. Marvento is a furnished shared home with a handful of private en suite rooms. You get a private bedroom and bathroom plus shared kitchen, living areas, laundry, and yard — without a leasing office, amenity fees, or a 200-unit hallway.

### How much do student apartments near St. Augustine usually cost?
St. Augustine’s average apartment rent sits near $1,850/month before utilities and internet. Once you add fees, deposits, furniture, and Wi-Fi, many one-bedrooms clear $2,000. Marvento private rooms run $1,195–$1,650 all-inclusive depending on term length; shared rooms start at $850 per student.

### Can Flagler students live here?
Yes — especially juniors, seniors, and students approved to live off campus. Flagler requires most first- and second-year students to live on campus; if you are eligible for off-campus housing, 3530 Datura is about a 10-minute drive from the King Street campus.

### Do I need a Social Security number or co-signer?
No. We lease without requiring a U.S. SSN or a domestic co-signer — a common blocker at traditional complexes.

### Can I use a student loan housing stipend?
Often yes. Ask your school’s financial aid contact to include living expenses when certifying your loan. We provide a signed lease and monthly receipts. Marvento is not a lender and is not affiliated with Sallie Mae.

### How do I reserve a room?
Submit the availability form. We confirm within 24 hours and can hold a room remotely before you arrive.
