### Gaps that lose apartment-seeking students

1. **Broad “student apartments St. Augustine” intent has no dedicated hub.** Searchers typing “apartments near St Augustine FL” or “student housing St Augustine” land on a senior-heavy homepage or a pilot-only page. Flagler juniors bounce.
2. **Senior content dominates the sitemap** (adult family care, independent living, lifestyle support). Student surface area is thin outside two pages.
3. **“Coliving” is the brand word; “apartments” is the search word.** Students search apartments. The product is a furnished private room in a shared home — content must bridge that language gap without misleading.
4. **Homepage CTA is generic.** “Explore Our Homes” splits seniors and students. Student ads work better when the first screen is student-only.
5. **No nurture after inquiry.** Form → silence loses students still comparing RISE, Craigslist, and motels.
6. **Flagler is a one-line mention** on `/3530-datura`. Upperclassmen need their own narrative (semester timing, ~10 min to campus, all-inclusive vs splitting utilities).

### Competitive frame (how students currently decide)

```
Search "apartments St Augustine" / "student housing"
        │
        ├─ Complexes (RISE, Landing) → income proof, guarantor, unfurnished or setup fees
        ├─ Downtown houses (Flagler Student Housing) → high deposit, find roommates, utilities
        ├─ Motels / hostels near airport → temporary, no kitchen / no study desk
        └─ Marvento → furnished en suite, one rate, flexible term, no SSN/co-signer
```

**Winning argument:** You get apartment-level privacy (private room + private bath) without apartment-level friction (furniture, deposits, 12-month lock-in, guarantor).

## Content pillars

### 1. Find a room near campus or the airport
Product-led. Where students live relative to Flagler, KSGJ, Florida Flyers, groceries, beaches.

### 2. What it really costs to rent in St. Augustine
Objection-led. True monthly cost: rent + fees + utilities + Wi-Fi + furniture + deposits.

### 3. Move in without the paperwork trap
Friction-led. No SSN, no co-signer, remote lease, M1 visa hold, loan stipend documentation.

### 4. Live like a student, not a tourist
Lifestyle-led. Study desks, quiet hours, kitchen, laundry, community of peers — vs motel or empty unit.

## Messaging principles

1. **Bridge search language:** Lead with “student apartments / student housing,” then clarify “private furnished room with en suite in a shared home.”
2. **One number:** Always lead with all-inclusive from $1,195 (longer term) and show the tier ladder.
3. **Name the friction:** SSN, co-signer, furniture, 12-month leases — these are why students end up in motels.
4. **Be precise about location:** 3530 Datura · ~10 min Flagler · ~8–11 min KSGJ — not “close to everything.”
5. **Never pretend to be a 200-unit complex.** Scarcity of rooms is a feature when framed as “a real home, not a leasing office.”
