## Article

The rent on the listing is rarely the number that hits your account.

Students comparing **apartments near St. Augustine, FL** often anchor on base rent, then discover mandatory fees, utility deposits, internet installs, and a furniture run to Target. Flight students comparing motels get a simpler bill — and a worse place to study.

Here is a clear-eyed monthly comparison using figures already published on Marvento’s student pages, plus public market averages.

### What students under-budget

1. **Utility deposits** — new accounts often require deposits before the first kilowatt.  
2. **Internet activation** — install windows collide with move-in week.  
3. **Furniture** — a bed + desk + chair is not optional for coursework or ground school.  
4. **Roommate shortfall** — a four-bedroom downtown house looks cheap until one roommate drops out.  
5. **Early termination** — ending a 12-month lease when training finishes early is expensive.

### When a traditional apartment still wins

Choose a conventional apartment if you want full privacy (no shared kitchen), you can satisfy income or guarantor requirements, and you will stay long enough to amortize furniture. For Flagler students settling in for multiple years with local credit, that path can make sense.

### When a furnished all-inclusive room wins

Choose a furnished shared home when you need:

- A **single predictable number** for a loan stipend or parent budget  
- Move-in within days, not weeks of setup  
- Flexible term length (month-to-month through 12 months)  
- No SSN / co-signer paperwork  

Marvento’s student home at **3530 Datura Street** prices private en suite rooms from **$1,195/month** on a 10–12 month term up to **$1,650/month** month-to-month, with shared rooms from **$850 per student**. Utilities, Wi-Fi, and weekly housekeeping of common areas are included.
