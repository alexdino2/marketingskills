## Article

If a St. Augustine apartment portal rejects you for lacking a **Social Security number** or a **U.S. co-signer**, you are not alone. That screening model assumes domestic employment history. It was not built for M1 visa flight students, new arrivals, or out-of-state students without a local guarantor.

### Why traditional complexes say no

Most conventional communities near U.S. 1 ask for some mix of:

- A U.S. Social Security number for credit screening  
- Verifiable income around **3× monthly rent**  
- A domestic co-signer / guarantor when income is thin  
- Application fees stacked per roommate  

Fail any one gate and the “available now” unit disappears — which is why so many incoming students default to motels.

### What “lease without SSN” actually means

It does **not** mean free housing or skipped identity checks. It means the landlord uses an alternative underwriting path: passport / visa documentation, school enrollment, deposits defined in the lease, and remote signing.

Always read the lease. Ask what documents are required, what is refundable, and when keys are released.

### Practical checklist before you wire a deposit

1. Confirm the exact legal name on the lease matches your passport.  
2. Get move-in date, rate, term length, and what “all-inclusive” covers in writing.  
3. Ask how the room hold works if a visa is delayed.  
4. Save the signed lease and monthly receipts if a school loan stipend requires documentation.  
5. Never pay a personal account that is not named in the lease instructions.
