## Section: What your VA benefit gives you

**H2:** What your VA benefit gives you

**Intro:**  
You earned this. Here’s what it unlocks on most VA purchase loans.

1. **$0 down payment**  
   Finance up to 100% of the purchase price on most VA purchase loans. No down payment required in those cases.

2. **No private mortgage insurance**  
   Unlike FHA or low-down conventional loans, VA loans never require PMI — often $100–$300+ per month back in your budget.

3. **Competitive VA rates**  
   VA loans are typically among the lowest rates available, backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

4. **Flexible credit path**  
   We work with credit scores down to 580 in many cases. A rough credit history doesn’t automatically end your VA path.

5. **Reusable entitlement**  
   Use your benefit more than once — for a move, an upgrade, or (when rules allow) a second property.

6. **Surviving spouses welcome**  
   Eligible surviving spouses can use the VA loan benefit too. Ask us to confirm eligibility.

## Section: Extra benefits for Florida veterans

**Eyebrow:** Florida veterans

**H2:** Extra benefits for Florida veterans

**Intro:**  
Florida is one of the most veteran-friendly states in the country. On top of your federal VA loan benefits, you may qualify for state and local property tax savings.

- **Homestead exemption** — Exempt up to $50,000 of assessed value on your primary residence.
- **Disabled veteran property tax discount** — Veterans 65+ with combat-related disabilities may receive a discount equal to their VA disability rating.
- **100% disabled veteran exemption** — Permanent service-connected total disability may mean a full homestead property tax exemption.
- **No state income tax** — Florida has zero state income tax, so VA disability and retirement pay stretch further.

*Tax benefits depend on eligibility and local rules. We help you buy; your county appraiser and VA confirm tax status.*
