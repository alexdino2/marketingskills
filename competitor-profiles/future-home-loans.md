## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Independent broker access to many wholesale lenders + speed + veteran-owned service; “deal savers” for borrowers declined elsewhere.

**Target audience**: Florida (esp. First Coast) homebuyers, veterans/military, investors, realtor/builder partners; secondary: recruiting experienced LOs/brokers nationally.

**Positioning angle**: Local veteran-owned broker that combines wholesale pricing flexibility with high-touch ops — not a call-center retail lender.

**Key messaging themes**:
- No lender fees for veterans (third-party fees still apply) — VA page + homepage
- Speed (14-day CTC) — about + VA
- Multi-lender choice (30–40+) — homepage + careers
- Broker+ recruiting (comp, pricing, ops support) — join-our-team
