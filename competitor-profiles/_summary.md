## Competitor landscape overview

Future Home Loans competes in three rings at once: (1) **local Jacksonville / First Coast brokers** fighting for realtor referrals and Google Business Pack (North Star Mortgage Network, Edge Home Finance LOs, First Commerce Financial’s expansion); (2) **national retail lenders with JAX branches** (Movement, Fairway) selling speed and brand; (3) **national VA specialists** (Veterans United, plus Navy Federal / USAA adjacent) that own VA education and head-term search. FHL’s distinctive stack — veteran-owned identity, VA fee waiver, multi-lender brokerage, 14-day CTC, and a Broker+ recruiting flywheel — is strongest against retail and national VA factories on *local trust + flexibility*, and strongest against boutique brokers on *scale and productized ops*.

## Key takeaways

1. **Local broker war is personal-brand + GBP**, not just domain SEO. NSMN (Nathan Young) and Edge (Douglas Wilkerson) win deals FHL never sees on the website — elevate named FHL VA specialists.
2. **Veterans United sets the VA narrative nationally**; FHL’s winning counter is *local + veteran-owned + fee waiver + multi-lender + anti-call-center proof* (already in testimonials).
3. **First Commerce is the sharpest emerging content threat** on the First Coast — physician niche and long-form JAX pages are gaps in FHL’s public site.
4. **Movement/Fairway win on realtor speed narratives and paid reach**; FHL should weaponize 14-day CTC + broker pricing in realtor enablement, not only consumer ads.
5. **Broker+ recruiting is a real strategic advantage** none of the local boutiques match — capacity growth is a competitive moat if LO experience stays strong.
