## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- Category brand and trust for VA
- Review and content moat
- Productized digital experience (app)
- National media/listicle presence

### Weaknesses
- Call-center / national scale can feel impersonal (FHL reviews explicitly contrast vs national lenders)
- Not a multi-lender broker — cannot shop overlays the way FHL can
- Less local Florida market intimacy (insurance, homestead, First Coast nuances) on primary UX
- Non-VA / investor products not a strength

## Competitive Implications for Future Home Loans

**Where they're strong vs. us**: National VA demand capture, SEO, brand, review count, digital UX.

**Where we're strong vs. them**: Local Florida presence; veteran-owned brokerage identity; no-lender-fee veterans offer; multi-lender flexibility; Non-QM/investor; realtor-local relationships; speed claims with local accountability.

**Opportunities**: Position as “the Florida veteran-owned alternative to the national VA factory”; capture refugees from national-lender friction (already evidenced in FHL testimonials); dominate local VA + Jacksonville modifiers.

**Threats**: Veterans United education content shapes borrower expectations before they meet a local broker; paid competition on VA keywords.
